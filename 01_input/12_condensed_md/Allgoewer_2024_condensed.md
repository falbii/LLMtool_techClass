- **Article:** “Cost-Effective Locations for Producing Fuels and Chemicals from Carbon Dioxide and Low-Carbon Hydrogen in the Future”; Ind. Eng. Chem. Res. 2024, 63, 13660−13676; published July 29, 2024.
- Study locations: Iceland, The Netherlands, Spain, Chile.
- Products: Fischer−Tropsch fuels, methanol, methane, dimethyl ether, ammonia, urea, olefins, aromatics.
- Time horizons: near future (by 2035), long-term future (post-2035).
- Hydrogen production: alkaline water electrolysis, proton exchange membranes, solid oxide electrolyzer cells.
- Carbon feedstock: low-temperature solid adsorption direct air capture (LT DAC); high-temperature aqueous absorption DAC; point-source capture.
- Long-term future cost ratios vs fossil-based counterparts: 1 to 6.5 times.
- Cost per ton of CO2 eq avoided: 150 and 750 €/t.
- Net-zero carbon emissions around 2050; global warming limit 2 °C.
- CCU technologies estimated to potentially reduce approximately 10% of current global CO2 emissions.

[PAGE 2]

- Scope: full product supply chain from CO2 and hydrogen production to transportation of final products to Basel, Switzerland.
- CO2 sources: low-temperature solid adsorption direct air capture (LT DAC), high-temperature aqueous absorption DAC (HT DAC), point-source flue gas capture such as cement production site.
- Electrolysis: alkaline water electrolysis (AEC), proton exchange membrane electrolysis (PEM), solid oxide electrolysis (SOEC).
- Timeframes: near future (by 2035); long-term future (post-2035 but by 2050).
- Reference unit: one metric ton of final product; for Fischer−Tropsch (FT) fuels, olefins, and aromatics, one metric ton of the mix.
- Products assessed: Fischer−Tropsch fuels, methanol, methane, dimethyl ether, ammonia, urea, olefins, aromatics.
- Locations: Iceland, The Netherlands, Spain, Chile; Chile aims to become world’s lowest-cost producer of green hydrogen by 2030 and top hydrogen exporter by 2040.
- MSP investment lifetime: 25 years.
- Plant operation: 8000 h per year, except first and second year at 30% and 70% of total capacity.
- Investment allocation: 30% two years before first year of production, 50% one year before production, 20% in first year of production.
- Debt to equity ratio: 1.5.
- Near-term future: efficiencies expected for CCU technologies at commercial scale.
- Long-term future scenario: plant built using well-established technologies, i.e., after 2035.

- Monetary values: 2020 euros.
- CAPEX scaling: cost curve method; scaling factor 0.7.
- No scaling applied to DAC and H2 electrolysis.
- Plant capacities and CAPEX adjusted to annual CO2 input of 360 kt.

- Climeworks aims to capture CO2 at multimegaton scale by 2030.
