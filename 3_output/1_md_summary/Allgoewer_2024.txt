═══ TECHNOLOGY 1: Fischer-Tropsch synthesis ═══

--- Year: 2035 (near future) ---
**Process Description:**
- Converts syngas (CO + H₂) to liquid FT fuels (jet fuel/diesel product mix)
- Operated as downstream unit of either RWGS or electrochemical reduction (ER) of CO₂
- Reference CO₂ input capacity: 360 kt_CO₂/y
- Plant lifetime: 25 y; operating hours: 8000 h/y (year 1: 30% capacity; year 2: 70%)
- Delivery point: Basel, Switzerland

**Inputs (via RWGS route):**
- CO feed from RWGS unit: capacity corresponds to 229.3 kt_CO/y upstream
- H₂ from electrolysis (AEC/PEM/SOEC)

**Inputs (via ER route):**
- CO feed from ER unit: capacity corresponds to 225.3 kt_CO/y upstream

**Outputs:**
- FT fuels (RWGS route): 116.4 kt_FT-fuel/y at reference scale
- FT fuels (ER route): 114.4 kt_FT-fuel/y at reference scale
- LHV of product: 44.3 MJ/kg (jet fuel/diesel)

**CAPEX (at 360 kt_CO₂/y input, reference):**
- RWGS route — FT synthesis unit: 100.1 M€ (min 44.9, max 195.8 M€)
- ER route — FT synthesis unit: 98.8 M€ (min 44.4, max 193.4 M€)
- CAPEX scaling factor k = 0.7 (applied to FT unit)
- CAPEX includes compressors, heat exchangers, separation units

**MSP (near-future, LT DAC, all locations/electrolyzers):**
- CCA range: 543–1969 €/t_CO₂eq,av (RWGS and ER routes combined with other fuels/chemicals)
- Iceland lowest CCA near-future

**Fossil benchmark:**
- 2020 avg fossil price: 456 €/t (jet fuel/diesel)
- Fossil GHG: 4164 kg_CO₂eq/t_product
- Fossil comparator: 94 kg_CO₂eq/GJ_LHV and 80 kg_CO₂eq/GJ_LHV (EU RED)
- CO₂-based fuel combustion emissions considered carbon neutral until 2040

**LCA / GHG (near-future):**
- Near-future avg over all products/electrolyzers/locations: 17.1% higher GHG (1319 kg_CO₂eq/t_product) than long-term future
- Netherlands emits least; AEC most favorable electrolyzer

**MSP (long-term, LT DAC, baseline ranges across locations):**

- Netherlands products avg ~6% lower GHG than Iceland, ~50% lower than Spain, ~55% lower than Chile
